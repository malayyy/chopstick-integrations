How we work

A practical process that starts with your real operation and ends with a system your team can actually use.

  1. Understand

    We learn how your business operates, which tools you use and where the current process breaks down.

  2. Make

    We define the solution, technical approach, scope and delivery milestones before development begins.

  3. Measure

    We configure, integrate, automate and test the system against real business workflows.

  4. Again

    We deploy the solution, document the work and help your team take ownership.

Fair questions

Which CRM platforms do you work with?

Zoho is our core CRM platform. We also work with Close CRM, Salesforce and HubSpot, depending on the project requirements.

How does a project usually start?

We start by understanding the business problem, the systems involved and what you want to achieve. Then we define scope, technical approach and delivery milestones.

Do you work alongside in-house teams?

We work with your team and include documentation and knowledge transfer so your people can understand and operate the systems we build.

How do you price the work?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity and delivery requirements. We can structure projects around defined milestones and agreed deliverables.

Can you work with our existing tools?

We work remotely with businesses in different locations. The working arrangement depends on the project and communication requirements.
