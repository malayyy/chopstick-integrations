PROJECT CASE STUDY

Connected Zoho operations and analytics

Connecting CRM, Books, Desk, workflow automation and analytics so commercial and service teams work from consistent information.

CHOPSTICK INTEGRATIONS · Project overview · 7 NOVEMBER 2026

Challenge. When CRM, finance and support tools operate independently, teams repeat data entry and lose context as records move between departments.

Connected application layer

  • Zoho CRM: customer, lead and deal context.
  • Zoho Books: finance and transaction context.
  • Zoho Desk: support activity and service history.
  • Zoho Flow and Deluge: workflow orchestration and custom business rules.
  • Zoho Analytics: consolidated reporting and operational visibility.

Engineering details

Integrations need explicit field mapping, stable record matching, duplicate-safe upserts, retry handling and clear ownership of each data field. Analytics needs consistent definitions so dashboards reflect the underlying operational reality.

From activity to visibility

Reporting can connect lead source, owner activity, follow-up status, pipeline movement and sales outcomes. Managers should be able to move from a metric to the records behind it.

Core stack: Zoho CRM · Books · Desk · Flow · Deluge · Analytics
