PROJECT CASE STUDY

SWASH cleaning operations platform

Zoho CRM and Field Service Management workflows for a cleaning-services operating model.

CHOPSTICK INTEGRATIONS · Project overview · 7 NOVEMBER 2026

Project scope. Zoho CRM and FSM implementation work for the SWASH cleaning platform, connecting customer information to service delivery operations.

Operational model

A service business needs more than a customer database. It must connect customer records, service requirements, job scheduling, field execution and status updates that office teams rely on.

System focus

  • CRM records as customer and commercial context.
  • Field-service jobs and scheduling in the FSM workflow.
  • Clear status progression from booking through completion.
  • Connected information to reduce repeated data entry.

Implementation principle

Model the service lifecycle first, then configure CRM and FSM around it. This avoids forcing dispatchers and field teams into a generic sales pipeline that does not match service delivery.

Core stack: Zoho CRM · Zoho FSM · workflow configuration
