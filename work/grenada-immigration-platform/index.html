PROJECT CASE STUDY

Grenada national immigration platform

A public-sector immigration platform connecting application intake, case operations and document workflows across Zoho CRM, WorkDrive and Zoho Creator.

CHOPSTICK INTEGRATIONS · Project overview · 7 NOVEMBER 2026

Challenge. Immigration workflows coordinate applicant information, supporting documents, internal review and communication across multiple stages. Fragmented records make case status and document retrieval difficult to track.

System architecture

  • Zoho CRM: central operational records and case workflow.
  • Zoho WorkDrive: structured document storage and retrieval.
  • Zoho Creator Portal: public-facing experience for applicants and external users.

Engineering focus

The work connected public-facing intake with internal CRM records and document handling. The architecture was designed around clear record relationships, controlled access and a consistent view of application progress.

Why this is complex

Public-sector systems have multiple user groups, sensitive records, document-heavy processes and workflows that cannot be treated as a simple lead pipeline. The solution must account for how information enters, who can access it, how it moves through review and how documents remain associated with the correct case.

Core stack: Zoho CRM · Zoho WorkDrive · Zoho Creator Portal
